Introduction Who We Are Legal Basis Information We Collect Your Rights

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KOG (Kleiner Oil Group) collects, uses, stores, and manages information through the KOG Bot and within KOG-managed communities.

KOG is based in and operated from the United Kingdom. Our community includes members primarily located in the United Kingdom, the European Union, and the United States, and this policy is written to address the data protection laws relevant to those regions.

By interacting with the bot, participating in KOG events, or joining KOG-managed environments, you acknowledge the data handling practices described in this policy.

2. Who We Are

KOG (Kleiner Oil Group) is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact details are provided in Section 12.

We do not currently have a formal Data Protection Officer (DPO), as this is not a legal requirement for an organisation of our size and processing activities. This will be reviewed if our processing activities change.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following lawful bases, in accordance with the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR (for EU-based members), and the Data Protection Act 2018:

  • Legitimate Interest - maintaining community safety, managing event leaderboards, quota tracking, and enforcing server rules.
  • Consent - when you voluntarily submit information or trigger bot commands.
  • Compliance & Safety - protecting the community from ban evasion, harassment, and security threats.
  • Legal Obligation - where we are required to retain or disclose data to comply with applicable law.

Where our legal basis is legitimate interest, we have considered whether that interest is overridden by your rights and freedoms, and concluded it is not, given the limited and community-specific nature of the data involved.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Bot Interaction & Event Data

To track medals, event participation, and quotas, we store the following data tied to your unique Discord User ID:

  • User Identification: Discord User ID (userId).
  • Participation Metrics: eventsAttended, eventsHosted, merits, trainingsAttended, squadAttended, squadMerit, squadHosted.
  • Platform Attributes: Assigned Discord roles (for quota and permission verification).

4.2 Moderation and Safety Records

To protect our community and enforce rules, authorised staff may record:

  • Moderation actions (warnings, strikes, kicks, and bans).
  • Timestamps and moderator notes explaining action reasons.
  • Relevant evidence (such as message excerpts or screenshots of rule violations).

4.3 Public Data Checks

For verification and safety compliance, our automated systems or staff may inspect publicly available information, including:

  • Activity in KOG-managed channels.
  • Public Roblox profile data, group memberships, and public badges/inventories, to check for restricted or blacklisted items.

Note: We do not collect IP addresses, payment details, private direct messages, or non-public personal information.

5. Automated Checks and Human Review

Some of our public data checks (Section 4.3) are performed automatically. Where an automated check could in some circumstances result in action against you - including failing background checks, and in rare cases, removal from the group. You may contact us via Kleiner Internal Affairs Department (KIAD) (Section 12) to query or appeal any decision you believe was made in error.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or commercially distribute your data.

Data is handled internally. Moderation and background check records may only be shared with affiliate administrative branches (such as QSOC, QIAD, or KIAD HR) on a strict need-to-know basis, for legitimate community safety purposes.

Data will not be shared with external, non-affiliated third parties unless required by law.

The bot operates on Discord's infrastructure, and by using Discord you are also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which govern how Discord itself processes and stores data on its platform. We do not control, and are not responsible for, Discord's own data handling practices.

7. International Data Transfers

Because we operate on Discord's infrastructure, data may be processed or stored on servers located outside the UK or European Economic Area (EEA), including in the United States.

Where this occurs, transfers are made under the safeguards Discord itself has in place (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms, as described in Discord's own Privacy Policy). We do not maintain separate international transfer agreements beyond those already governing our use of the Discord platform.

8. Data Retention Policy

We do not hold personal data indefinitely without justification.

  • Operational & Event Data (medals, merits, stats): We retain certain operational data indefinitely to ensure users do not lose progress or historical records. This includes event attendance, merits earned, hosted events, and other activity-based information.
  • Moderation & Safety Records: Warning logs, strikes, and ban records are retained for as long as they are needed to prevent to ban evasion and maintain community safety.

9. Your Rights & Data Requests

9.1 UK and EU Residents

If you are located in the UK or EU, under the UK GDPR / EU GDPR you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access - view much of your stored event data directly via the /mydata bot command, or request a full copy of your records from administration.
  • Right to Rectification - request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to Erasure - request deletion of your non-essential event and merit data at any time.
  • Right to Restrict Processing - request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Object - object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint - you have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), reachable at ico.org.uk. If you are based in the EU, you may complain to your local data protection authority instead.

Note on Moderation Data Erasure: Requests to erase active moderation records, ban logs, or ongoing disciplinary files may be lawfully refused where keeping that data is strictly necessary to protect server safety, prevent ban evasion, or satisfy legal obligations, under UK/EU GDPR Article 17.

Data requests will be processed within 30 days of receipt.

9.2 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, and to the extent the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) applies to our processing, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used (see Section 4).
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to the same moderation-record exceptions described in Section 9.1.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is no "opt-out of sale/sharing" mechanism required. You may still submit any request under this section using the contact details in Section 12.

10. Age Requirement & Children's Privacy

The KOG Bot and KOG services are intended for users who are 13 years of age or older, in line with Discord's own Terms of Service and U.S. COPPA requirements.

Note for EU users: Some EU member states set the age of consent for information society services higher than 13 (up to 16, under GDPR Article 8). If you are located in an EU member state with a higher age threshold, you should have parental or guardian consent to use the bot until you reach that threshold.

11. Data Security & Breach Notification

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data we hold. In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users and, where legally required, the ICO or relevant supervisory authority, without undue delay.
